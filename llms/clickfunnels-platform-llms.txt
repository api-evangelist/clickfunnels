# ClickFunnels

> ClickFunnels is a SaaS platform for building sales funnels, landing pages, websites, e-commerce stores, and marketing automation. It enables entrepreneurs and businesses to create complete online sales experiences without coding.

## Company Information

- [ClickFunnels, Inc.](https://www.clickfunnels.com): Founded in 2014 by Russell Brunson and Todd Dickerson
- [Boise, Idaho, USA](https://www.clickfunnels.com): Headquarters
- [Support Center](https://support.myclickfunnels.com): Help docs, guides, and tutorials
- [Privacy Policy](https://www.clickfunnels.com/privacy-policy): ClickFunnels privacy policy
- [Terms of Service](https://www.clickfunnels.com/terms): ClickFunnels terms of service

## Pricing and Plans

- [Startup](https://www.clickfunnels.com/pricing): Entry-level plan for new entrepreneurs building their first funnels
- [Pro](https://www.clickfunnels.com/pricing): Mid-tier plan with expanded limits and advanced features
- [Funnel Hacker](https://www.clickfunnels.com/pricing): Premium plan with unlimited funnels, courses, and priority support

## Core Features

ClickFunnels is an all-in-one platform. Each workspace provides:

- **Funnels**: Multi-step sales flows (opt-in, sales, order, upsell, thank-you pages)
- **Sites & Landing Pages**: Full websites, blogs, and standalone landing pages
- **Products & Store**: Digital and physical product management with an e-commerce storefront
- **Courses**: Online course creation and delivery with student enrollment
- **Email**: Email marketing — broadcasts, templates, and automated sequences
- **Automations (Workflows)**: Event-driven workflow automation with triggers and actions
- **Contacts & CRM**: Customer data management, tagging, and segmentation
- **Community**: Member community spaces for courses and memberships
- **Appointments**: Scheduling and booking system
- **Analytics**: Funnel performance, revenue tracking, and reporting dashboards
- **Affiliate Center**: Affiliate program management with commission tracking
- **MessageHub**: Multi-channel messaging (email, SMS, chat)
- **Payments AI**: Intelligent payment processing and dunning
- **Countdown Funnels**: Time-limited urgency campaigns
- **Discounts**: Promotional codes and coupon management
- **Short Links**: Branded URL shortening
- **Opportunities**: Sales pipeline management
- **Survey Workflows**: Survey and feedback collection
